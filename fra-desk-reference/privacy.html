Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective 30 May 2026

Summary

FRA Desk Reference does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The app works fully offline.

Information we do not collect

The app does not request, access or transmit any of the following:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or any other contact information
  • Location (GPS or otherwise)
  • Photos, files, contacts, calendar, or other on-device content
  • Device identifiers used for advertising or analytics
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or telemetry

There are no accounts, no sign-up, no sign-in.

Data the app does use

The app reads forest indicator data from a database bundled inside the app at install time. This data is sourced from the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations' Global Forest Resources Assessment and is used solely to display charts and figures inside the app.

Network activity

FRA Desk Reference does not make any network requests. It does not contact any servers we operate, and it does not contact third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics providers.

Third-party services

The app does not integrate any third-party SDKs that collect personal data.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children or anyone else.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same URL with a new effective date.
