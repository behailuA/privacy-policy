The world's forests, in your pocket.

FRA Desk Reference is an iPhone and iPad app for browsing FAO's Global Forest Resources Assessment data — official, country-by-country forest indicators for every reporting nation.

What's inside

Pick a country, pick a topic, and explore clean charts powered by FAO's authoritative dataset across 235 countries and the 1990–2015 reporting series.

  • Forest Area & Characteristics
  • Production
  • Biodiversity & Conservation
  • Protective Functions
  • Disturbance
  • Sustainable Forest Management
  • Economics & Livelihood

Data source

All figures are sourced from the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations' Global Forest Resources Assessment, the most authoritative global source of forest data, compiled from official national reports.
